Vyremaid

OSRS Clan Utility Bot

Vyremaid links each member's Discord account to their in-game RSN and keeps a clan role in sync automatically via Wise Old Man group membership — no manual role management. From there it covers the everyday utility stuff a clan actually needs: live hiscores and skill lookups, boss kill counts, a money-making guide filtered to what you personally qualify for, Grand Exchange prices and loot-split calculations, Skill/Boss of the Week tracking, clan-wide XP leaderboards, and live trackers for shooting stars and Tears of Guthix worlds.

What it does

Account Linking & Roles

Members link their RSN once with /setusername. The bot checks it against your Wise Old Man group and auto-assigns/removes a clan role — no admin busywork.

Hiscores & Stats

Full skill tables, single-skill lookups for all 24 skills, boss kill counts, combined EHP/EHB profile cards, and personal-best gain records via Wise Old Man.

Money Making Guide

!mmg shows methods filtered to what you personally qualify for, sorted by profit/hr — not a generic list you have to cross-reference yourself.

Prices & Splits

Live Grand Exchange prices, plus !split <item> <people> to instantly divide a drop's value across your team.

Shooting Stars

!stars shows active shooting stars filtered to what your Mining level can actually mine, hiding anything already gone stale.

Tears of Guthix

!tog surfaces the best crowd-confirmed worlds for this week's stream order.

SOTW & Leaderboards

Auto-detected Skill/Boss of the Week standings, plus clan-wide top XP/level gainer leaderboards over any time period.

Auto-Responses & Welcome DMs

Configurable trigger-word replies, and a customizable DM new members get automatically on join.

Quick setup

  1. Click Invite Vyremaid above and add it to your server.
  2. Position the bot's role above any role you want it to manage (Server Settings → Roles).
  3. Run !setgroup <your Wise Old Man group link> to link your clan's WOM group.
  4. Run !setclanrole @YourRole to pick which role gets auto-synced.
  5. Have members run !setusername <their RSN> — done.

Full walkthrough (including optional channel restrictions and WOM auto-refresh) is in the command reference PDF.
