Gear catalog (!buy / !gear / !equip)

Every buyable item, split by combat style. Weapon and ring are the only slots that reduce task time (Speed); every other slot carries a Defense rating instead, which reduces how much food a Medium/Hard slayer task burns through (see Formulas). Buying via !buy is never level-gated — only !equip checks the level requirement.

Special/reward items (!equip)

Named items that break the normal "one stat per slot" rule — some grant both a Speed and a Defense bonus at once. All untradeable (no !sell value) and reward-only: drops (Cyclops defenders), slayer points shop purchases (Slayer helm), or quest rewards — not buyable via !buy.

Toolbelt (!buy / !gathershop)

Pickaxe/hatchet tiers — a separate equip slot from combat gear, always active regardless of your combat style, and not buyable with gp: earned via Gathering points instead. Speed reduces gathering time for their matching skill only.

Gathering points shop (!gathershop)

Gathering points are a separate currency from gp and slayer points, earned passively while gathering (any skill, any resource — chance is normalized so the rate is a flat regardless of what you're gathering). Spend them here on toolbelt upgrades via !buy <item> [qty].

Processing recipes (!use)

Cooking, Crafting, Prayer, Fletching, and Smithing — all queued through !use, flat 2s/unit regardless of tier (unlike gathering, which gets slower at higher tiers). Recipes with more than one possible output prompt you to pick one via reaction when you start it.

Monsters & drop tables (!slaytask)

Slayer points shop (!buy / !slayershop)

Bought with slayer points (a separate currency from gp, earned only on full task completions) — !buy auto-detects a points-priced item same as it does gp gear/food. !slayershop just browses the list.

Quests (!quest / !quests)

One-time completions, fixed duration by tier, no partial credit for stopping early (same shape as group content below). Every quest awards Quest Points based on its tier on top of its own reward — QP has its own separate milestone reward track, shown below the quest table. Names/requirements/reward flavor are pulled from real OSRS.

Group content (!barbassault)

Not a shared/synchronized instance — "grouping" is purely the start gate. A recruitment post stays open indefinitely (no deadline) until enough players react, then everyone starts their own independent leg of the encounter at the same moment. Guaranteed fixed reward on completion, no partial credit for stopping early.
